# Privacy Policy & Data Deletion — Loop

Last Updated: September 30, 2026

## 1. Our Commitment to Privacy
Loop is built for trusted groups (families, roommates, friends) to keep each other accountable. We collect only what is strictly necessary to run your shared loops, routines, and emergency contacts.

## 2. Information We Collect
- **Account Data**: Name, email, timezone, and accessibility preferences (e.g. Simple Patient Mode, High Contrast AAA theme, Text-to-Speech settings).
- **Loop Data**: Items tracked (habits, medicines, chores, shared expenses) and timestamps of check-ins.
- **Emergency Contact Info**: Voluntary emergency phone numbers, physician details, allergies, and blood type recorded in Medicine Loops.

## 3. How We Use Data
- To coordinate real-time updates and reminders between authorized members in your private Loops.
- To alert caregivers when a scheduled medicine dose is missed.
- We **never sell**, rent, or monetize your health or personal data.

## 4. Account & Data Deletion
You retain complete ownership of your data:
- You may permanently delete your account, member history, and health notes at any time from within the app under **Settings > Delete Account**.
- Alternatively, email `privacy@loopapp.example.com` to request complete deletion of all records across our databases and backups within 30 days.
